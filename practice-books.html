About Practice Books

ILM Practice Books are intended to support students as they revise mathematical concepts and develop problem-solving skills. Structured practice can help learners build fluency, recognise common patterns and become more comfortable explaining their answers.

Book titles, class levels, contents, availability and prices may change. Contact ILM for the current catalogue and ordering information.
