About Regional Math Clubs

ILM Regional Math Clubs are a learning pathway built around collaboration and curiosity. Students can explore mathematical ideas, discuss different ways to solve a problem and learn from one another in a supportive environment.

The purpose of RMC is to create opportunities for students in different areas to learn mathematics collaboratively, discuss solution methods and build confidence. Suggested activity types include puzzles, number patterns, group challenges, practical mathematics and guided practice. These are programme activity ideas, not a confirmed timetable or list of active club locations.
