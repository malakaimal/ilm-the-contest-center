Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about programmes, admission and student services.

Getting started

What is ILM The Contest Center?

ILM The Contest Center is an educational initiative that provides mathematics learning opportunities, Olympiads and academic contests to encourage problem-solving and student achievement.

Who can participate in ILM programmes?

Eligibility depends on the individual programme or competition. Please read the relevant programme page and its latest official announcement for grade and location requirements.

How do I register as a student?

Open the Admission page, complete the student registration form carefully and submit it. Use accurate contact details so the ILM team can communicate with you.

Is registration open all year?

Registration periods can vary by programme. Check the Olympiads and Contests pages for current announcements and dates.

Programmes and competitions

What is the difference between an Olympiad and a contest?

An Olympiad focuses on mathematical thinking and problem-solving, while a contest may cover the subjects and format announced for that specific event. Always check the event details.

Where can I find the eligibility and competition details?

Visit the relevant Olympiads or Contests page. Official announcements should be treated as the final source for dates, grades, subjects, fees and participation instructions.

How will I know about upcoming events?

Check the ILM website announcements and follow the official contact channels listed on the Contact page.

Student account and results

How do I access the Student Portal?

Use the Student Portal link in the website navigation and sign in with the account details you registered. Do not share your password with anyone.

How can I check my result?

Open the Results page and follow the instructions shown there. Results will only be available after they have been officially published.

How can I verify a certificate?

Visit the Certificates page and follow the verification instructions provided. A certificate can only be confirmed if its record has been issued and published by ILM.

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check that you are using the correct registered email and password, then try again. If the issue continues, contact ILM using the details on the Contact page. Never send your password in a message.

Need more help?

How can I contact ILM?

Email ilmthecontestcenter@gmail.com or use the contact details and contact form on the Contact page.

Where can parents or schools ask questions?

Parents and school representatives can contact ILM through the official Contact page and include the programme or event they are asking about.
