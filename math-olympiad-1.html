About the Programme

ILM Math Olympiad 1 is designed to encourage students to understand mathematical ideas, strengthen number sense and apply classroom learning to interesting problems. The focus is on clear thinking and learning, not memorising methods alone.

Students are encouraged to read questions carefully, explore different approaches and explain how they reach an answer.

🔢

Core Concepts

Practise number operations, patterns, basic geometry and other age-appropriate mathematical ideas.

🧠

Logical Thinking

Develop reasoning skills by recognising relationships, comparing information and solving problems step by step.

🌱

Learning Confidence

Build confidence through practice, constructive feedback and a positive competition experience.
