About the Programme

ILM Math Olympiad 2 offers a further opportunity for learners who are ready to explore more involved mathematical questions. It encourages students to connect concepts, organise their thinking and try more than one strategy when approaching a challenge.

The programme aims to make advanced practice approachable, supportive and suitable for the learner's grade level.

🧩

Multi-step Problems

Practise breaking a larger question into smaller, understandable steps.

📐

Mathematical Reasoning

Explore patterns, relationships, geometry and number-based challenges.

🎯

Independent Thinking

Build the habit of checking solutions and choosing strategies thoughtfully.
