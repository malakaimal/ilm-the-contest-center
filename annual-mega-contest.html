About the Contest

The ILM Annual Mega Contest brings students together for a structured academic competition. It is intended to give learners an opportunity to apply their knowledge, practise careful thinking and take part in a positive, well-organised event.

Previous AMC-2026 planning information described an event for Grades 4–9 across Shangla. Families should use the current official notice to confirm the active session's eligibility and arrangements.
