02 • SUBJECTS & PAPER PATTERN

What will students be tested on?

The announced paper pattern contains 80 multiple-choice questions across two subject groups.

🧮

Mathematics & IQ

50 MCQs

Questions are intended to assess mathematical understanding, numerical skills, patterns and logical reasoning.

📖

English Grammar

30 MCQs

Questions focus on English grammar knowledge and language accuracy appropriate to the participating classes.

The detailed class-wise syllabus, sample/model paper, test duration and marking rules should be followed only when published in an official ILM notice. They are not specified here.
