ILM Talent Olympiad 2026

Competition information

Key details for students and parents. Any date or instruction not listed here will be shared in an official ILM notice.

📚

Subjects

English Grammar, Mathematics and IQ. Questions are intended to assess language skills, numerical understanding and logical reasoning.

📝

Test pattern

80 multiple-choice questions: 50 Mathematics and IQ questions, plus 30 English Grammar questions.

💻

Test mode

Online, home-based test. Students should follow the official test instructions and use a reliable internet connection and suitable device.

🎓

Eligibility

Students currently studying in Classes 5, 6, 7 or 8 may apply, subject to the final ILM registration notice.

🏅

Recognition

Participation certificates and cash prizes are planned for the competition. Prize details and award criteria will be announced by ILM.

📨

Application

Submit the ILM admission form with accurate student and guardian contact details. Follow the payment and document instructions shown on the form.

Important: The registration fee is PKR 370. Test date, duration, detailed syllabus, payment account information and prize distribution rules must be confirmed through the latest official ILM announcement before payment or participation.
